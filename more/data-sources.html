Market Data Sources

This page explains how market information should be presented in Goa Market Pulse.

Source transparency

Each market rate should show its source, unit, location and last updated time whenever available.

Typical categories

  • Goa Bagaydar / agriculture rates
  • Vegetable market rates
  • Construction material rates
  • Petrol, diesel and LPG rates
  • Gold and silver rates

Rate disclaimer

Displayed rates may vary by market, quality, tax, transport, dealer, date and availability. The app should not present estimates as confirmed official rates.
