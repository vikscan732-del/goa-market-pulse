Help & FAQ

Quick answers for common Goa Market Pulse questions.

How often are prices updated?

Prices are updated according to the available source and your app’s data schedule. Always check the displayed update time.

Are the prices guaranteed?

No. Market prices can change by location, quality, supplier and time. Verify important rates before making a purchase.

Why is a page not loading?

Check your internet connection, try refreshing, and confirm that the linked source website is available.

Are government links official?

The app should link to official government domains wherever possible. Confirm the domain before submitting personal information.

How can I report an incorrect rate?

Use the Contact Us page and include the product, displayed rate, date, source and a screenshot if possible.

Does the app require an account?

The More pages are designed without an account requirement. Any future account feature must be explained separately.
